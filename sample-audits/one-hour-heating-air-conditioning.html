Worth fixing
Your website does not include LocalBusiness structured data (JSON-LD schema). This is a recognized technical signal that helps Google understand your business name, phone number, address, service area, and hours in a machine-readable format, and it reinforces the connection between your website and your Google Business Profile.
Do this: Add LocalBusiness schema to your homepage. Include your business name, phone number, address, service areas (Phoenix metro cities), hours (24/7), and a link to your Google profile. This is a best practice for local service businesses.
Minor

Do this: Consider using a clean URL (onehourheatandair.com/west-valley) on your Google profile and handle UTM tagging via server-side redirects or Google Tag Manager. This is purely cosmetic but improves user trust.
